Services

I help organizations and development teams to bridge the gap between fast and secure. By shifting security to the left and building robust Internal Developer Platforms, I enable development teams to ship stable software releases - fast, frequently, securely.

DevSecOps Link to heading

Security shouldn’t be a final hurdle but a shared responsibility baked into every commit. I help you automate trust across your entire Software Development Lifecycle.

  • Continuous Security (CI/CD): Building automated pipelines that catch vulnerabilities, bugs, and compliance risks before they reach production.
  • Infrastructure as Code (IaC): Provisioning secure, scalable, and audited infrastructure using industry-standard automation.
  • Threat Modeling: Identifying risks early in the design phase to minimize downstream remediation costs.
  • Team Enablement: Guiding the transition to a “Security-First” culture through hands-on mentorship and architectural leadership.

Platform Engineering Link to heading

Modern cloud-native environments are complex. I build the toolchains that shield your developers from infrastructure fatigue.

  • Internal Developer Platforms (IDP): Creating self-service portals that allow developers to deploy and manage environments independently.
  • Custom Tooling: Developing specialized resources to replace fragile “workaround” scripts with production-grade solutions.
  • Developer Experience (DX): Streamlining the path from local dev to production, reducing onboarding time and cognitive load.
  • Kubernetes & Cloud-Native: Architecting and optimizing containerized workloads for reliability and performance.
